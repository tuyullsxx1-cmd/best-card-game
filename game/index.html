EMBERFALL

Duel of the Last Flame

Two Keepers. One flame. Twenty life each — and only one walks away with it.

Each turn: gain 3 ember, draw 1 card, play up to 2 cards — each into a slot of the battle line.

Flame deals damage. Ash gains shield. Spark draws cards or gains ember.

Battle line: Vanguard favours Flame (+1), Hearth favours Ash (+2), Ember favours Spark (+1).

Kindle: play two cards of the same suit in one turn and the second one gets +2.

Chains: Flame then Ash Quenches (+2 shield); Ash then Spark Ignites (your next Flame +2); Spark then Flame Flares (+1 damage, draw 1).

Ember Surge: every third Flame you play in the duel feeds the brazier and returns +1 ember.

Second wave: 24 cards now — Ember Lash counts double toward Surge, Cinder Echo repeats your last Chain, Wildfire ignores shield, Ashen Mirror reflects 2 damage, Slot Swap earns its slot bonus anywhere, Chain Link fires the next Chain twice.

Stances: the Warden is AGGRESSIVE 🔥 while its life is above 10 and DEFENSIVE 🛡 at 10 or below — read the badge beside its life before it strikes.

Draft: after every duel, win or lose, pick 1 of 3 cards to add to your deck for the next duel. Your deck grows; the Warden's does not.

Read the Warden's intent before it strikes — then answer it.
